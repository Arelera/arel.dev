# Arel

## Register

brand

## Users

Chinese learners choosing videos, stories, and practical ways to understand
them. Many arrive from search on a phone and want a clear answer before
committing to a long article. They may use either simplified or traditional
Chinese and read in bright daylight or a dim room.

## Product purpose

Publish useful, original Chinese-learning guides and introduce Moyu Chinese
and Miaozi where they help the reader take the next step. Moyu is the main
business priority. The site has no personal identity or resume content.

## Brand personality

Friendly, curious, plainspoken. The hand-drawn cat gives the site personality;
the writing sounds like a helpful person explaining a specific example.

## Design principles

- Keep arel's own identity while learning from Moyu's warmth and Miaozi's
  approachable teaching layouts.
- Use one rounded font, Quicksand, with system Chinese fallbacks.
- Give prose, Chinese examples, practical steps, and product links different
  visual roles. Leave breathing room without turning every paragraph into a card.
- Use a warm light theme and a neutral dark theme, with restrained blue,
  terracotta, and soft green accents.
- Preserve clear titles, immediate answers, natural writing, and visible
  simplified/traditional script support.

## Avoid

Decorative borders and dividers, shadows, badges, tiny slogan headings,
invented Chinese decoration, fake product screens, generic marketing copy,
and identical grids of icon cards. Promote only Moyu and Miaozi.

## Accessibility

Keyboard-operable controls, clear focus, readable contrast in both themes,
reduced-motion support, stable images, and layouts that work on narrow phones.
